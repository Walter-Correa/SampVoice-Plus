SampVoice Plus - Installer Guide
==================================

Requirements
------------
- Windows 7 SP1 or newer (32-bit x86 game process)
- GTA San Andreas 1.0 + SA-MP 0.3.7 (R1, R2, R3-1, R4, R4-2, R5-1 / R5-2-MP)
  or SA-MP 0.3.DL-R1 (including DL-R1-2-MP) already installed.
  The installer will block installation if SA-MP is missing.
  Downloads:
    SA-MP: https://www.sa-mp.mp/downloads/
    or use the open.mp Launcher: https://open.mp/downloads/launcher

Installation Steps
------------------
1. Run SampVoice-Plus-Setup.exe.
2. Choose your installer language (auto-detected from Windows; 15 supported).
3. Confirm your GTA San Andreas folder and complete the wizard.

What the Installer Does
-----------------------
- Installs sampvoice.asi into your GTA San Andreas root folder, places uninstall
  metadata and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt inside sampvoice-plus\, and creates the
  "Uninstall SampVoice Plus" shortcut in your GTA San Andreas root folder.
- Checks for an existing ASI loader and installs Ultimate ASI Loader (dinput8.dll,
  MIT license) only if no ASI loader is present.
- Detects legacy SampVoice (setup or manual) and lets you back it up into
  sampvoice-plus\backup\ (restored on uninstall) or remove it permanently.
- Never modifies bass.dll, vorbisFile.dll, or vorbisHooked.dll. Reinstalling or
  updating SA-MP later will NOT break SampVoice Plus.
- Silent switches: /LEGACY=remove (permanent legacy removal) and
  /LEGACYCONFIG=remove (also delete legacy Documents\GTA San Andreas User Files\sampvoice\).
- Full license notices are provided in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt at the root of
  SampVoice-Plus-Client-Installer.zip (and installed into sampvoice-plus\).

Windows SmartScreen Note
------------------------
Because the installer is not digitally code-signed, Windows SmartScreen may show
an "Unknown publisher" warning. Click "More info" -> "Run anyway" to proceed.

In-Game Controls & Settings
---------------------------
- Press F11 in-game to open the settings menu (hotkey and 15 live languages
  can be changed in the General tab).
- Configuration & log path: Documents\GTA San Andreas User Files\SAMP\sampvoice\

Uninstalling
------------
Open Windows "Installed apps" -> "SampVoice Plus" -> Uninstall, or double-click
"Uninstall SampVoice Plus" in your GTA San Andreas root folder.
All files added by the installer are removed and backed-up files are restored,
leaving your GTA San Andreas folder in its previous state.
